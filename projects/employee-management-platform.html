Internship

Employee Management Platform

Organisation
Algobrain AI
Role
AI & ML Intern
Dates
Aug 2023 – May 2024
Location
Surat, India

Overview

Workspace: Algobrain AI’s in-house platform for attendance, projects, tasks and leave, with face-recognition check-in.

The problem

We studied tools like Zoho and Jira. Off-the-shelf systems had weak task management and employee monitoring, limited security and poor reporting, and none used AI for attendance. The company wanted one place to run its day-to-day work.

What I built

  • Face-recognition check-in and check-out with a live session timer. Admins enrol each employee with a short face video.
  • Attendance and working-hours tracking with location capture, plus an admin dashboard of hours and attendance reports.
  • Role-based access control: separate admin and employee panels, with admins managing users, roles and permissions.
  • Projects and tasks: project management, task assignment and a Kanban board (backlog, to do, review, done).
  • Leave requests with admin approval, holidays, an events calendar, announcements and notifications.

Screens

  • Admin panelAdmin dashboard Live headcount, ongoing projects and working hours per employee.

  • Admin panelAttendance calendar Pick any employee and see their presence day by day.

  • Admin panelLeave requests Review, approve or reject leave with a message to the employee.

  • Admin panelProject management Create projects, assign contributors and leads, edit or delete.

  • Admin panelEvents calendar Schedule company events and meetings, then edit or delete them.

  • Admin panelAnnouncements Send notices to everyone and see who has read them.

  • Employee appFace-recognition check-in The session timer starts once the camera recognises the employee.

  • Employee appMy projects Role, deadlines and days left on every project at a glance.

  • Employee appTask board Kanban across backlog, to do, review and done.

  • Employee appNotifications Admin notices land in each employee’s feed.

Challenges

  • ProblemLow-quality laptop cameras produced hazy images, so faces often weren’t detected.

    FixLet employees check in with their phone camera when their laptop camera wasn’t good enough.

  • ProblemIP-based location was often wrong, which broke location-dependent check-in.

    FixSwitched to GPS geolocation from the device for accurate positions.

My role

Project Lead. I owned the backend in Flask and MongoDB and built and styled the web pages, working with a fellow intern on the front end. During training I also built a CSV upload tool that produced insights with machine-learning models.

Tech stack

  • Python
  • Flask
  • MongoDB
  • JavaScript
  • HTML / CSS
  • Bootstrap
  • Face recognition
  • RBAC

Outcome

Rolled out as the company’s own workspace, and the team worked about 20% more efficiently.

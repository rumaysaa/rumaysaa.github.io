Overview

A full-stack AI platform that uses a local LLM and RAG to generate personalised study material for each learner. It was my M.Sc. Computer Science final-year group project at Technological University Dublin.

My role

Project Lead on the M.Sc. final-year group project, 2025–2026.

What I built

  • Personalised study material generated by a local LLM with RAG: MCQs, flashcards and recap cards, and mock tests.
  • An AI Tutor for concept explanations and instant summaries.
  • Upload of documents and handwritten notes, with handwriting read through OCR.
  1. 01Study modules
  2. 02PDF reader with highlight-to-notes
  3. 03AI chat with summaries and knowledge-gap detection
  4. 04Three-level recap cards that unlock progressively
  5. 05Generated MCQ quizzes with a leaderboard
  6. 06Pomodoro timer
  7. 07Progress dashboard: overall progress, modules completed, day streak, average test score
  8. 08Light and dark themes
